## Handy tip
Go on, be a smartass but tons of people don't know this. Fuck I didn't for ages and I get paid to.

### From the OWA 
- hit your profile icon at the top right
- click open another mailbox
- enter the name of the shared mailbox
- bam, another tab another mailbox
