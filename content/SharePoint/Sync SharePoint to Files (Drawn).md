## Another dang handy one.
What you need:
- A SharePoint library
- A Microsoft account with OneDrive
- OneDrive on your PC, that is signed in
- A PC
- To know where your SharePoint Library is, URL is best


Go to the URL, sign in if you have to, you're now looking at your SharePoint Library yay.

Click the 3 Dots, click sync, it'll ask if it can sync, say yes.


![[Sync SharePoint to Files (Drawn).png]]

### Done
It wont be up top with your OneDrive, it'll be under the quick access with This PC, and Google Drive and OneDrive
