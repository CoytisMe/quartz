## Got a backup from Skype?
wtf is a .json file? how do I look at this?

### SKYPE PARSER

[Microsoft has the answer here](https://support.microsoft.com/en-au/skype/how-do-i-export-or-delete-my-skype-data-84546e00-2fef-4c45-8ef6-3a27f83242cc), but I'll do with pictures.

### Step 1
Click that link, expand the first option (Export or Delete etc etc) and scroll down to ‘To Extract the .tar’. In part 5 of that section the word ‘[Viewer](https://go.skype.com/skype-parser)’ is blue, clicking it will download the tool. You can also just click that word here because I got your back.

![[View a Skype Backup.png]]

### Step 2
Download the .tar, extract the .tar. Click on the index.html

![[View a Skype Backup-1.png]]

### Step 3
Upload the .json, hit load
