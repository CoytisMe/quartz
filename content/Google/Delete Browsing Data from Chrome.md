## Web not Paging?

### Step One
Hit the 3 dot menu at the top right of Chrome and choose 'Delete Browsing Data'.

![[Delete Browsing Data from Chrome1.png]]

### Step Two
In the resulting menu make sure the time is set to 'All time' and 'Cookies and other site data' and 'Cached images and files' are selected and hit delete data.

![[Delete Browsing Data from Chrome.png]]

### Step Three
Sign every fucking webpage back in, sorry forgot to mention it does that.
