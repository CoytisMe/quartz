A simple man's simple guide to the 
fucktopian, ass-backwards 'process' devised by the godkings at Google to transfer gmail data from one Workplace Tenancy to another.

There's probably a better way, there's always 10 ways to do anything with these chucklefucks, but this is what I did.

Capitalised words and phrases are for emphasis and your safety and / or demarcations of the steps I fucked up the first time. 

1. Sign into the GLOBAL ADMIN of the DESTINATION tenancy.
2. Data > Data import and export > Data Export
![[14-05-26.png]]
3.  Scroll down to 'Import data from another Google Account
	   - Choose BUSINESS GMAIL and click import
	![[14-05-26-1.png]] 
4. Enter the the email address of a GLOBAL ADMIN of the SOURCE TENANCY.
	   You're going to want to put the email you want to migrate over in here. Don't
	   ![[Migrating Between Workspace Tenancies.png]]
	   
5. Sign into the GLOBAL ADMIN of the Source tenancy. Go to Gmail, find this email.
   ![[Migrating Between Workspace Tenancies-1.png]]
6. Accept the authorisation, I could get screenshots you'll work it out.
   
7. Go back to the import screen we're up to step two now. Uploading an import data map.
   ![[Migrating Between Workspace Tenancies-2.png]]
   It sounds scary but the example these is literally it; Source Email and Target email.
   Make it and upload it
   
8. Easy as fuck from here so they going in the same picture.
   ![[Migrating Between Workspace Tenancies-3.png]]
