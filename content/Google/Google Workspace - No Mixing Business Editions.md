# Google Workspace - No Mixing Business Editions

## What I thought
Google was allowing us to mix licenses across different users

## My Evidence
Fuck if I know I thought I'd heard it somewhere.

## The rule
Google Workspace **doesn't let you mix Business editions in one tenant.** If the tenant is on Business Standard, you can't add a cheaper Business Starter seat for one person.

## The ask that hit it
Contractors needed access to the company's Google Drive, and some wanted Gmail.

## Options
| Need | Option |
|---|---|
| Drive files and Docs only | Share links, or a free **Cloud Identity Free** user ([[Google Workspace Cloud Identity]]), which can open shares and use Google Docs |
| Gmail on the company domain | A full seat on the tenant's **existing** edition. There's no cheaper tier available |
| Freeing up a seat | Look for full-licence accounts that don't need mail (e.g. a service/admin account) and drop them to Cloud Identity Free |

## Tip
Make your own admin account a Cloud Identity Free user too (2FA'd and in the password manager), rather than burning a paid seat.
