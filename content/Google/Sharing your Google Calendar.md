## Want people to know when you're acting busy?

### Step 1 & 2:
- Head to [calendar.google.com](https://calendar.google.com/)
- Find your calendar on the list to the left
- Smash them 3 dots
- Hit 'Settings and sharing'

![[Sharing your Google Calendar.png]]

### Step 3:
- You're now in Calendar Settings
- Head down to 'Shared With'
	- Here you'll see the previous times you did this and forgot. You can change settings if you want
- Hit '+Add people and groups'

![[Sharing your Google Calendar-1.png]]

### Step Final (4)
- Type in the name of the calendar receiver
- Click on send.
- Done and done
