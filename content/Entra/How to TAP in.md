**Step 1:**
Head to the MS Admin, go to Identity (Entra)

**Step 2:** 
In Entra admin? Hit Users, click on the user you want

![[Temporary Access Pass.png]]

**Step 3:**
Click on the user, in the user panel click on authentication methods.

**Step 4:**
Select Temporary Access Pass, set your duration, default is 60 mins. Make sure it's not on single use (they always break) and hit add

![[Temporary Access Pass-1.png]]

**Step 5:**
Copy the TAP, be careful, once you close this window you can't see it again./

**Step 6:**
Sign in anywhere with your TAP, if it asks for a PW, put a wrong one in and it will ask if you want to use the TAP instead.
![[Temporary Access Pass-2.png]]
