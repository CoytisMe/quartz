# Australian Government Budget Totals

Quick reference for total government expenditure figures — useful for contextualising individual spending line items (e.g. "X% of total budget").

> **Latest full year: 2023–24**

---

## Federal (Commonwealth)

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Total expenditure (2023–24 actual)** | ~**$703–715 billion** |
| As % of GDP | ~26.6% |

*The 2024–25 budget projected $734B. The 2023–24 Final Budget Outcome reported total expenses in the high $600s–low $700s range. Use ~$700B as a round working figure.*
