## Key Differences

| Feature | G8 ($1549) | G9 ($1399) | Winner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $1549 | $1399 | G9 (-$150) |
| Battery | 45Wh | 48Wh | G9 |
| Display Brightness | 300nits | 400nits | G9 |
| Thunderbolt 4 DisplayPort | DP 2.1 | DP 1.4 | G8 |
| TPM | Firmware TPM 2.0 | Discrete TPM 2.0 | G9 |
| Fingerprint Reader | Touch integrated | Match-on-Chip in power button | G9 |

**Summary:** The G9 is $150 cheaper and wins or matches on most specs. The only meaningful advantage of the G8 is DisplayPort 2.1 on its Thunderbolt 4 port (vs DP 1.4 on the G9) — relevant if connecting a high-res/high-refresh external monitor via USB-C. For general use, the G9 is the better value.
