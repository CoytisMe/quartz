Sets Office Apps to only update once a month as opposed to the minute they're ready.

Suggested by TriConvey support to slow down office updates and avoid issues with Word. Seems pretty harmless to enable.

## Steps
- M365 Admin Panel
- Settings
- Org Settings
- Microsoft 365 Installation Options
