# Teams Premium vs Copilot for Meeting Notes
All the dude used Teams Prem for was the transcription and summary, this was a slam dunk.

## The situation
A user had Teams Premium on top of Business Standard, and the only thing they used it for was **meeting transcripts and summaries**.

## The comparison
Copilot covers transcription and summaries, and does more on top. Prices are AUD per user per month, as seen in Oct 2026:

| | Current | Swapped |
|---|---|---|
| Business Standard | $23.13 | Business Standard + Copilot combo pack |
| Teams Premium | $16.52 | |
| **Total** | **$39.65** | **$38.74** |

The Copilot combo came out **slightly cheaper** than Bus Std + Teams Premium.

## Doing the swap
1. Assign the Business Standard + Copilot combo licence.
2. **Remove Teams Premium** once the combo is assigned, so they're not paying for both.
3. Tell the user whether it's monthly or annual.
