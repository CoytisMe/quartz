## Our detects password.excel
Sends us an email, we have to tell the client to fuck it off because it's dumb

## Best Practice?
Move all of the passwords in your stupid spreadsheet to a password manager, we use 1password and we like it?

## Oh? Don't want to do that?
What? 1Password costs money and you're not down for that? Fine

## The barely acceptable alternative
Change the filename to not have 'password' it in, and password protect it.

File > Info > Protect Workbook > Encrypt with Password > Set a password

## Write that fucker down
There is no 'forgot my password' for this, you forget it and data is gone.
