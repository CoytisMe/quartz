We've discussed a password management solution for you in the past but I figure now's as good a time as any to get it down in words and hopefully get the ball rolling on deployment. The system we recommend and use ourselves is 1Password. In the simplest terms it does exactly what you'd expect; saves passwords, provides autocomplete for websites in your browser, essentially replaces what Chrome and Edge do already.

​What sets 1Password apart and justifies it's spend is a couple of nice little extra features but primarily the enhanced security.

Security:
Without getting too technical with it, 1Password uses layers and methods of encryption that results in your data being close as it can feasibly get to being cryptographically un-hackable. It's a level of encryption that hackers wouldn't even attempt to penetrate unless there were, billions, state secrets, or nuclear codes on the other side.

End-to-end encryption and the use of a 128-bit secret key that's kept offsite required for login to any new devices prevents data interception and credential theft. The password data is visible only to the account holder, as admin you won't even be able to see your own employees personal vaults unless you take control of their account.

To prevent most of not all 'Human Error' issues all 1Password extensions and software has a 10 minute idle lock. Meaning that if it's not used for more than 10 minutes it requires you enter a password in order to access any of your data. Admittedly this can be grating a times but ​PCs left unattended with passwords ready to autocomplete can be a serious issue, I'd go as far as to far this feature alone is the reason to get 1Password.

The last security point is also a management feature: Control. Each user gets their own account with their own Personal Vault for storage. You then create shared vaults and decide who has access to them. You can see and control who sees what, who's done what etc. If someone needs to be let go you can remove their access to everything instantly.
Features:
1Password comes primarily as an browser extension that takes over from Chrome or Edge in terms of autocomplete, saving new passwords etc.

You can save a vast array of different types of data securely into the system as well. credit card details, secure notes, uploaded documents.

A feature we use a LOT lately is saving and autocompleting OTPs (One Time Passwords). Those google authenticator, 6 digit scrolling numbers can be managed within the browser so no more pulling out your phone

The extension also has a very quick and accurate search so you can get what you're after with basic keywords then hit open and fill for it to take you to the page and sign you in. This combined with the Control + Shift + X keyboard shortcut to open the extension lately I've mostly stopped using bookmarks in Chrome as it's quicker and easier to use 1Password.

Pricing:
For your team's current size the pricing is pretty amazing. The Team Starter Pack is $19.95 USD a month, so about $30 AUD, that gets you 10 accounts, all sorted and ready to go.

It is worth mentioning if you have further growth planned, once you need an 11th user you need to swap to the Business plan which is $7.99 USD per user, quite a jump up.

Deployment:
Should you decide to go ahead with this the process is very simple; We make an account, fire it up with your billings details. We then go person to person around the business, export all saved password in Chrome and / or Edge. Make an account for them, install the extension, log it all in and import the data. Good to go.

Where possible we like to do the account set ups with people as it's at this point that the secret keys are generated so we want to ensure that we have the only copy. Also it gives us a change to make sure their comfortable with the password requirement, it tends to throw people off.

Very little business disruption, realistically 10-15 minutes per person. We'll show you how to log in as admin so you can manage shared vaults and such as you need, alternatively you can let us know what you want and we'll make it happen.
