Limbs + Riser usually measured at 25" riser, same with draw weight.

Limbs come in short medium and long

'Medium' limbs 68@25 = 68" Bow if on a 25" rise

Count down if riser is shorter.
Medium Limbs on a 17" riser = 60" bow

Limb Lengths for this purpose (Not Advertised)

Short = 41"
Medium = 43"
Long = 45"

## Draw Weights
Every inch less than weighed riser = 1-2lbs increased draw weight
