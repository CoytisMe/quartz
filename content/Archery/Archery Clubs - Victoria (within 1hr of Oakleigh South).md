## Very Close

- **Moorabbin Archery Club** – Turner Rd Reserve, Turner Rd, Highett
  Victoria's largest target archery club. Open 7 days. Outdoor + indoor range. Olympic training venue.
  🔗 [mac.asn.au](https://www.mac.asn.au)

- **AIM Archery Club** – 73 Grange Rd, Cheltenham
  Indoor club, largest indoor range in Victoria. Open late some nights.
  🔗 [aimarcheryclub.com.au](https://aimarcheryclub.com.au)

- **Waverley City Archers** – Freeway Reserve, Mulgrave
  One of Victoria's most recognised clubs.
  🔗 [waverleycityarchers.org.au](https://waverleycityarchers.org.au)

---

## Less Close

- **Angel Archers** – Emu Plains Reserve, Balnarring (Mornington Peninsula)
  Target, field & clout.
  🔗 [angelarchers.org](https://angelarchers.org)

- **Southern Cross Archery** – 142 Cranwell St, Braybrook
  Small target club, weekly Come 'n' Try sessions.
  🔗 [southerncrossarchery.com](https://southerncrossarchery.com)

- **Kew City Bowmen** – Hays Paddock, Kew
  Outdoor target club, beginner courses most weekends. No booking needed.
  🔗 [kewarchery.com](https://www.kewarchery.com)

- **Frankston Archery Club** – Cranbourne Secondary College, Brunt St, Cranbourne North, 3977
  🔗 [archeryvic.org.au/club-information](https://archeryvic.org.au/club-information/)

- **Box Hill Archery Club** – R.H.L. Sparks Reserve, Box Hill
  Regular beginner courses in morning blocks.

- **Yarra Bowmen** – Bulleen Park, Bulleen
  Recurve, compound & crossbow. Open 7 days.

- **Western Melbourne Field Archers** – Laurie Emmins Reserve, Laverton North
  Field archery. Meets 2nd and 4th Sunday each month.
  🔗 [westernmelbournefieldarchers.com](https://www.westernmelbournefieldarchers.com)

## Way Less Close

- **Diamond Valley Archers** – Yarrambat Park, Yarrambat
  Victoria's biggest club. Full range of disciplines (target, field, indoor, 3D).
  🔗 [dva.asn.au](https://www.dva.asn.au)

- **Heidelberg Archers** – Ambrose Archery, 25 Orr St, Heidelberg Heights
  Indoor club (12 lane, 18m range).

- **Yarra Valley Archers** – Yarra Valley Archery Park, Launching Place
  Target, field & indoor.
