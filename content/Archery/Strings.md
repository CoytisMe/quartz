## General
- AMO = Length of Bow
- String should be ~4 inches shorter for recurve
- Confirm if length listed on string is corrected for this, don't believe it is
## Materials
 ### D97
 - High Strength, Low Stretch
 - Specifically BCY DynaFLIGHT 97
- 100% SK75 Dyneema
